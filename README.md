### 👋 About me

I'm a **full-stack web developer** from Vienna with a strong focus on **front-end engineering, UI/UX and AI integration**.
I take products from idea to production: design system, typed front end, API, database, deployment — and I care about the details users actually feel: speed, clarity and polish.

- 🚀 Currently building **[ggez.gg](https://ggez.gg)** — a League of Legends stats & AI-coach platform, no ads, no account
- 🧠 Exploring **AI tooling**: multi-agent workflows, LLM-powered browser extensions and assistants
- 🎨 Shipped **15+ client projects** — websites, web apps and brand designs ([case studies](https://christophbarton.com))
- 🌍 Working in **German & English**
